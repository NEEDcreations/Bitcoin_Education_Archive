🗑️ User Data Deletion

Bitcoin Education Archive — bitcoineducation.quest

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open bitcoineducation.quest
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Go to Settings → Account
  4. Choose Delete account
  5. Type your username to confirm. If a sats balance is still on the account, confirm that it is forfeited
  6. Sign in again if asked, then enter a fresh authenticator code if you use one

This permanently deletes your account and the data listed in the privacy policy, including your profile, points, badges, tickets, and forum posts that nobody else replied to. Posts other people replied to stay up with the author and body replaced by [deleted]. Faucet abuse logs, payout records, charity donations, and referral records are kept. A hashed record of the sign-in identity is kept so a new account cannot reset a ban, a sats block, a faucet cap, or a referral.

Option 2: Email us

Send an email to info.603btc@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data" and include the username. Deletion from the app is immediate. An email request is handled as soon as we can confirm the account.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your user profile and username
  • Points, rank, and streak data
  • Orange Tickets on your profile
  • Badges and quest progress
  • Forum posts nobody else replied to. Posts with replies from other people stay, with the name and text replaced
  • Push notification tokens
  • Any data linked to your Facebook, Google, Twitter, or GitHub login

What We Don't Store

We do not store your Facebook password, access tokens, or private social media data. We only receive your name/email for authentication purposes.
