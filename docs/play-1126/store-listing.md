## Full description

Bitcoin Quest is a free, gamified app for learning Bitcoin. Read a curated library of topics, complete quests, and track your progress as you go.

The app is for people 18 and older. Kids and Classroom features stay on the website only and are not part of this Android app.

What you can do:

• Study Bitcoin topics and complete learning quests
• Earn XP, badges, and optional promotional sats rewards (tokenized rewards only; not an exchange, and not stored Bitcoin we custody for you)
• Join community features such as chat, a forum, and optional peer-to-peer listings
• Watch educational videos on Timechain TV (YouTube may still show its own ads)

You can browse much of the library as a guest. A free account is needed for signed-in features such as quests, chat, and rewards. Reward payouts, when available, go to a Lightning address you provide and are subject to eligibility rules, limits, and the in-app 18+ requirement.

Premium checkout is not offered in the Android app. If you already have Premium from the website, your existing benefits may still apply; new purchases are made on the website, not here.

This app is an educational product from 603BTC LLC. It is not a bank, broker, or cryptocurrency exchange.
