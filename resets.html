Bitcoin Quest resets, timelines & odds

Every clock and every odds figure below is the one the server enforces, read from the code on November 20, 2026. Eastern times are Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5) until the clocks spring forward on March 14, 2027. A job locked to UTC then shows one hour later on an Eastern clock. A job locked to America/New_York stays on the same Eastern clock.

Reminder resets

Opt in only. Each reset has its own switch, and every switch starts off. A reminder goes out about an hour before that reset. If that hour is inside quiet hours (10 PM to 8 AM in your time zone, unless you change it), it is sent when quiet hours end, once per reset.

BIP Breakthrough (Bit Devs Socratic Seminar)

One real Bitcoin Improvement Proposal is featured each week, the same one for every player. The week starts Monday at 12:00 AM Eastern (America/New_York), so it does not move with daylight saving.

How to complete a week: use your daily spin on 7 Eastern calendar days in a row inside the same week, Monday through Sunday. Only the free daily spin counts. Bonus spins do not. Miss a day and that week cannot be completed, and the count starts over the next Monday.

Day 7 reward: the 7th spin lands on the 💎 Rare prize for sure, you collect that week's BIP badge (one per BIP), and you wear the Core Contributor chat title for 7 days.
