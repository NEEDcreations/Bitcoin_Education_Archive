Bitcoin Quest resets & timelines

Every clock below is the one the server enforces, read from the code on November 9, 2026. Eastern times are Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5) until the clocks spring forward on March 14, 2027. A job locked to UTC then shows one hour later on an Eastern clock. A job locked to America/New_York stays on the same Eastern clock.

Reminder resets

Opt in only. Each reset has its own switch, and every switch starts off. A reminder goes out about an hour before that reset. If that hour is inside quiet hours (10 PM to 8 AM in your time zone, unless you change it), it is sent when quiet hours end, once per reset.
