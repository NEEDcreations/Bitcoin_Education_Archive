⚠️ Anti-Abuse Policy: We employ device fingerprinting, IP-based rate limiting, server-side point validation, and behavioral analysis to detect faucet abuse. Specific protections include:
  • Device fingerprinting — a hashed hardware/canvas fingerprint is generated at claim time to detect multiple accounts on the same device (max 2 accounts per device)
