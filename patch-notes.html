Nacho's Sorting Ceremony

November 2–5, 2026
Latest
NEW
New players answer three quick questions and get a suggested way to play: Lite, Guided, Balanced, or Everything. Switch anytime from the bar at the top of home. Your XP and badges count in every mode, and Learn and Explore only show what that mode includes. After you pick, the Experience section folds down and opens again.
NEW
Badges are grouped into 11 collections. Finish one for bonus XP, Satoshi's Favor points, and a title you can wear. Each badge also climbs Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond. Higher tiers pay Orange Tickets, and tiers you already earned are credited.
NEW
Timechain TV Watch Party adds sats to a shared pot for every minute you watch. The pot sits up high, larger, and you can drag it. When the pot fills, viewers who watched at least 30 minutes split it. Tap Still watching? so your time counts. New accounts say hi in chat and watch Timechain TV before they can claim sats.
NEW
Keep a 7-day spin streak for a Bit Devs Breakthrough, and share it when it hits. Star up to two favorite apps. A bell on home lets you choose push alerts, which stay off until you turn them on. Tap your XP to open the leaderboard.
NEW
Browse past Satoshi's Favor epochs. Nacho answers Bitcoin easter eggs in chat and leaves your message up. When a faction wins the race, home hangs a champion banner. The Nook shop's bundles and prices got a refresh. Lightning claims show a flat 10 sat fee before you confirm, so a 100 sat claim arrives as 90.
FIX
If you don't have a username, the app asks you to pick one. A pinned chat banner scrolls to that message. Accept on the terms popup works, favorite buttons on home open again, and DMs open without crashing. Your rank share card and the Difficulty History table fit on a phone.

Double XP, No Asterisk

October 26 – November 1, 2026
Previous
NEW
Double XP now applies to every way you earn XP. The daily bar labels the full, half, and quarter sections so you can see what each stretch pays.
NEW
Search any player on the XP, Daily Streaks, Quests, and Top Predictors boards and see their real rank, even outside the top 25. The top 10 on XP, Quests, and Price Prediction win Orange Tickets every week and every month. The quest card has a Go to task button for your next step.
NEW
Cipher sats wins land in their own balance, with a bigger prize ladder including a 5 sat tier. Claim that balance from Inventory or Settings, on its own timer. Matching keys and ciphers stack into one row with a count, like 7F ×3. The terms page lists the odds.
NEW
Boss History sits at the bottom of the Raid screen and shows when each fight ended. Bosses arrive at 1 PM Eastern on the first of the month, the card shows HP left, and chat messages count toward Chat-Charger. Global Chat gives you a heads-up before Satoshi's Favor ends.
NEW
Friends who reach Maxi move your meetup tier, and the Invites card shows that progress plus the XP you earn per invite. Share a rank, a milestone, or a streak. A share post carries one link, your referral link, and the tagline no longer repeats the site name. Articles pay XP, and passing Scholar or Protocol Expert pays a big bonus with a certificate link. Nacho can summarize a Bitcoin book and point you to a legal copy. A batch of history documentaries joined Timechain TV.
FIX
Trivia shows the explanation after you answer, and a guest answer does not earn XP. Quiz Me in Nacho Mode asks a question again. The daily wheel shows only the prize you actually won. GGs posts have a celebrate button, and a badge name there opens what it means and how to earn it. Your profile shows your real join date, invites count when someone signs up, usernames use an underscore instead of a space, and home, DMs, and leaderboards open faster.

The Today Card Takes the Throne

October 22–25, 2026
Previous
NEW
Home opens on a Today card with Satoshi's Favor, your daily checklist, and the rest of your quests. Say hi in Global Chat for XP and an Orange Ticket. The card still says Say hi! after you've said hi, and Flex counts as done after 5 in a day. Watch 10 minutes of Timechain TV. Replying to someone in Global Chat can now earn you bonus XP. Daily XP is full through 500, half through 900, then a quarter until 1,000.
NEW
Nacho answers live Bitcoin questions with the number, the source, and a topic link, and he can point you to the right place in the app. He follows you on every page. Drag him anywhere, or tuck him into the deer button. Quote of the Day rotates through sourced quotes.
NEW
Start a conversation on a topic page. You earn XP, Nacho can answer in the thread, and Global Chat links the new post. A link back opens the paragraph you started from. On a computer, Browse topics opens a full-screen list with search. A More tools button beside chat opens AI Tools, Metrics, and Leaderboard.
NEW
Push notifications now work, with per-category controls in Settings. They stay off until you turn them on. Devices that were already registered stay on unless you had turned them off. Add an authenticator and you can remember this device for 30 days. Delete your account from Settings, or add an email to a guest or Lightning sign-in. Invite a friend from the home page. When they reach Maxi, you both get 50 orange tickets. Double XP starts when you buy it and doubles daily activity for an hour. Buying again adds another hour. On a tip day, I sent the tip counts once the tip is confirmed.
NEW
Send a key to a peer. If they open a matching cipher with it, you both win the same prize. Locked ciphers are easy to spot, gifts have new sounds, and you can rearrange inventory. Sats go to Wallet of Satoshi, Blink, ZBD, Speed, and other Lightning addresses. Unclaimed faction points show again, and you can pick a faction. That choice sticks. Your all-time Satoshi's Favor total shows on that panel. Nacho announces when Favor goes live, shouts when someone starts a PlebTalk thread or a Marketplace listing, and badge earns show up in GGs.
FIX
Jump into PvP when someone is waiting in the lobby. Marketplace listings can be posted, edited, and removed again. Beats hides strong language unless you choose to show it, and a track that's been taken down stays hidden. Timechain TV Music keeps Bitcoin and freedom songs on the right stations. Quiz and trivia rewards are checked before they pay out. Education topics were refreshed with current facts. Nacho is back to his classic look. Google sign-in works if you were browsing as a guest. The share card says Let me teach you about Bitcoin.

Previous
NEW
