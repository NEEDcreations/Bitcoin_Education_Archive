Faster Loads + Timelines

November 20, 2026
Latest
PERF
Rolling out soon after November 25: the app should feel seconds faster on phones, especially on a first visit. New visitors will no longer load the app twice, images will be much smaller, features will load only when you open them, and pages will stop jumping while they load. Return visits after an update should stay quick, the app code will be smaller, and the app will do less background work.
NEW
Resets, Timelines & Odds now lists the full odds for the daily spin, gifts, locked ciphers, Satoshi's Favor and the raffle, plus the weekly BIP Breakthrough schedule. Open it from the home link, or at resets.html.
NEW
The raffle now has an Official Rules page, and the Terms are updated to match. They spell out the rules, the odds, and how prizes are paid. Read them at raffle-rules.html and terms.html.
NEW
New players, including guests, now answer a few questions first and get a recommended way to play. If you start as a guest, your profile is kept if the app updates mid-signup, and Member since shows the right date.
NEW
Timechain Arcade adds 10 new games, for 21 in all, including HODL Cycle, Lightning Pinball, RoboBTC 2140, Fiat Fighters and Consensus Clash. A New badge marks the newest ones. A few game names, images and links were fixed, and Sat Arcade starts when you tap Play.
FIX
Official chat buttons such as Open PlebTalk are tappable again. Accept stays on screen for the PlebTalk and Lightning Mart rules on phones. The Satoshi's Favor info tip stays on screen.
NEW
Coming soon: the partnership popup will show only once, Daily Spin will show your current streak, a spin you leave mid-turn can be resumed, and the sats pot tip will explain that more watchers raise the rate, with a link to share.

Hot Streaks Are Back

November 19, 2026
Previous
NEW
Trivia battles now show a score bar and round dots after each question, and the end screen lists both scores with Rematch and Leave. Rematch takes you both back to the lobby. Buttons have labels, with a little more room on phones, and the countdown uses your opponent's checked name. You can earn the Hot Streak and Unstoppable badges again from your real win streak. The battle name screen is simpler: one field, Enter starts the fight, and the card no longer blocks taps.
FIX
Battles no longer stall if the question list has not loaded on your device. A round you both miss draws a new question instead of repeating, and questions do not repeat in the same match. They mix topics when they can, and 25 trivia answers were corrected. Practice limits reset at midnight Eastern, not on your device's date. Win and played badges follow finished matches counted by the server. If you leave a tied match, your win streak resets. You can only have one battle going at a time with the same person. Playing as a guest does not award streaks, badges, or XP, and guests do not see claimable sats. A reserved guest battle name is turned down with a clear message. If you later create an account, guest progress from before 7:30 AM Eastern on November 19 still counts the usual way on the weekly and monthly boards; progress after that stays off those boards and is not claimable. Your all-time score is your earned XP again. Cosmetic badges no longer add Satoshi's Favor.
