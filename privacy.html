7. Cookies & Local Storage

We take a minimal, privacy-first approach to browser storage. Here is an exact breakdown of what we store and why:

Cookies

We set one first-party cookie under normal operation:

  • googtrans — set only if you use the Google Translate feature, solely to remember your selected language. It is a functional cookie with no tracking purpose. If you do not use translation, this cookie is never set.

Firebase Authentication may set a short-lived __session cookie to manage your sign-in state. This is an HttpOnly, first-party session cookie. It contains no personal data beyond an opaque auth token and is cleared when you sign out or it expires.

We do not use:

  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Third-party analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • Fingerprinting or supercookies

Because we do not use non-essential cookies, you are not required to give cookie consent under GDPR/ePrivacy rules. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to beyond functional operation.

Browser localStorage

The majority of your in-app state is stored in your browser's localStorage — entirely on your device, never transmitted to us unless you create an account. Examples of what we store locally:

  • Preferences: theme (dark/light), audio settings, language, visited channels, notification preferences
  • Session state: anonymous user ID (btc_anon_uid), pending username/email during sign-up, streak data
  • Game & progress state: badges earned, PVP win history, daily spin timestamps, Satoshi's Favor data, quiz answers
  • Lightning wallet: your wallet connection method and NWC relay URL (if provided). These are stored only locally and are never sent to our servers. They are transmitted only directly to your own Lightning node or wallet when you initiate a payment.
  • Nacho AI: question history, friendship level, trivia scores, and closet items — all local only

You can clear all localStorage data at any time from your browser settings, which will reset app preferences and cached progress. If you have a signed-in account, your server-side profile (XP, badges, wallet balance, posts) is unaffected.

Service Worker Cache

The app operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA). A service worker caches app assets (scripts, stylesheets, images) on your device for offline access and faster loading. This cache contains only static app files — no personal data. You can clear it via your browser's "Clear site data" or "Clear cache" function.

9. Content Moderation & Filtering

All user-generated content (Global Chat messages, forum posts, DMs, usernames, articles) is filtered client-side before submission. No content is sent to external moderation services. Filters include:

  • Profanity filter — 100+ blocked words with leet-speak normalization (e.g., "f*ck", "sh1t" detected)
  • Hate speech & racial slurs — 27+ racial/ethnic slurs blocked across all surfaces
  • Sexual content — 30+ explicit terms and phrase patterns blocked
  • Self-harm detection — phrases indicating self-harm intent are blocked in public chat and flagged in DMs
  • Threat detection — violent threats ("I will kill you", "I know where you live") blocked via regex patterns
  • Political & religious extremism — political party names, extremist slogans, and religious hate terms blocked in public spaces
  • PII protection — phone numbers and email addresses are hard-blocked in Global Chat (allowed in DMs with confirmation)
  • Link filtering — URLs are stripped from Global Chat messages
  • Spam detection — ALL CAPS, repeated characters, and excessive links are flagged
  • Username validation — ASCII-only enforcement prevents Unicode/homoglyph bypass attempts

Filtering is performed entirely in your browser. No message content is sent to external moderation APIs or third-party services. Blocked messages are never stored in Firebase.

Firestore Data Structure

Sats claim data is stored in the following Firebase subcollections under your user document:

  • sats_withdrawals — individual claim records (amount, truncated invoice, timestamp, status)
  • sats_daily — daily aggregate tracking (amount claimed per day, claim count)
  • daily_points — server-side daily point award tracking (total awarded per day, enforces 500/day cap)
  • faucet_stats — global faucet statistics (total distributed, active claimants)
  • faucet_ip_log — IP addresses at claim time only (detects multi-account abuse per network)
  • faucet_fingerprints — device fingerprint hashes (detects multi-account abuse per device)
Privacy note: We intentionally do NOT store full Lightning invoices. Only the first 50 characters are retained (followed by "...") for basic audit purposes. This means we cannot reconstruct the full payment destination after the claim is processed.
