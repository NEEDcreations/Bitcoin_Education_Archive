Welcome to the Bitcoin Education Archive (Bitcoin Quest), which is live under https://bitcoineducation.quest

Bitcoin Quest is a Gamified Bitcoin Education app where users can convert in-game XP into real bitcoin.

The entire app was built by me and my Openclaw agent named Rufus using Claude Opus 4.6 from March-September 2026, and then me and my Grok Bot team of bots from October 2026-present.

The education featured in the website came from years of curating information on my Bitcoin Education Discord server. Now, it's all open source!
