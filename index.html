Your Saved Topics

You can Favorite any topic by clicking the ☆ Save star at the top of the topic. You can also Bookmark any message by clicking the 🔖 bookmark icon under each message.

All YouTube links are embedded for you and contain videos that supplement the information in each topic. Additionally, topics are filled with tweets that were posted on Twitter over the last few years (as well as some that are much older), and you can expand these tweets to view them in each topic. Furthermore, there are links that open websites, articles, podcasts and more that will lead you further down each rabbit hole of knowledge pertaining to Bitcoin.

Finally, you may see instances of conversational text within some topics. This is because all the information in these topics started in a Discord server where any users could chat and add their own educational resources. We kept some of the chat material in these topics to preserve the legacy of where it all started!

All the education in these topics will remain free and open source forever. Enjoy learning about the peaceful revolution that is Bitcoin! And we hope you share this app with your family and friends who may be curious about Bitcoin but don't know where to start!
