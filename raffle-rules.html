Orange Ticket Raffle Official Rules

Effective: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. PAYING DOES NOT IMPROVE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

These Official Rules apply to the monthly Orange Ticket Raffle in Bitcoin Quest, together with the Terms of Service (including Section 16g, Odds) and the Privacy Policy. If these Official Rules and the Terms conflict about the raffle, these Official Rules control.

1. Sponsor

The raffle is run by 603BTC LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company ("we" or "Sponsor"). You can reach the Sponsor at info@603btc.com. The raffle is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Apple, Google, or any app store or payment provider.

2. Eligibility

  • To enter and win, you must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) and have a Bitcoin Quest account in good standing that meets the age and account rules in Section 6 of the Terms.
  • Accounts that are banned, or have sats or withdrawals turned off, can't win and are left out of the draw. If that applies to your account, don't buy entries, because they can't win. Accounts for players under 18 and Classroom accounts can't enter or win.
  • The raffle is void where prohibited or restricted by law, including in any country or region under U.S. sanctions.
  • Sponsor's owners and their immediate family and household members can't win.
  • One account per person. Entries from duplicate or fake accounts, or from accounts that break the Terms, are void.

3. Entry period and how to enter

  • Each monthly raffle runs from just after the previous draw until 18:00 UTC on the 1st of the next month (the "Draw Time"). Entries go back to zero after each draw and don't carry over.
  • You get entries in Nacho's Nook using Orange Tickets. Orange Tickets are earned through free play, such as the daily spin, gift drops and quests. They can't be bought with money.
  • Orange Tickets that Premium members convert from XP beyond the 10 a day every player can convert can't be used for entries.
  • Each player can get up to 150 entries per UTC month, by every method combined.

4. Free entry by email

You can also enter without using any Orange Tickets. Email info@603btc.com with the subject "Raffle Free Entry" and your Bitcoin Quest username, from the email address on your account, before the Draw Time. Each eligible player who does this gets 10 entries for that month's draw, counted toward the 150-entry limit. One free-entry email per player per month. Free entries have the same chance to win as any other entry.

5. Prize

  • One prize per monthly draw: 21,000 satoshis (sats) of bitcoin.
  • The U.S. dollar value of the prize changes with the price of bitcoin. Its approximate retail value is the dollar value of 21,000 sats when it is paid. As one example, at a bitcoin price of $100,000 it would be $21.00. This example is not a promise of value.
  • The prize can't be transferred, swapped or paid in cash or any other form, except that Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if needed.

6. Draw and odds

  • One winner is drawn at the Draw Time by our server, using cryptographically secure randomness, from all eligible entries received.
  • Each entry is one equal chance. Your odds of winning are your eligible entries divided by all eligible entries in that draw.
  • Premium accounts and free accounts have the same odds per entry.

7. Winner notification and claiming

  • We tell the winner in the app within 7 days of the draw.
  • Before paying, we confirm the winner is eligible. We may ask for proof of age, identity or tax information, such as a Form W-9 where the law requires it.
  • The winner must respond and give a valid way to receive sats within 30 days of being told. Otherwise the prize is forfeited and we redraw an alternate winner from the remaining eligible entries. The alternate winner is told and must claim under these same rules.
  • We arrange payment with the winner, normally over the Lightning Network. If the prize pool can't pay a win right away, the win is kept and paid later, in the order it was won.

8. Taxes

The winner is responsible for any taxes on the prize. Where the law requires it, we will issue a tax form, such as a Form 1099, for the prize's value.

9. Fair play, changes and cancellation

  • We may disqualify anyone who cheats, uses bots or multiple accounts, tampers with the game or breaks the Terms, and void their entries.
  • If fraud, a technical failure or anything else outside our reasonable control affects the fairness of a draw, we may cancel or pause it. In that case we may hold the draw among the eligible entries received before the problem started, and we will post a notice in the app.
  • We may change these Official Rules for future draws. A change never affects a draw that has already taken place.

10. Release and limits of liability

To the extent the law allows, by entering you release Sponsor from claims arising from the raffle or the prize, except where caused by our gross negligence or willful misconduct. We are not responsible for lost, late or incomplete entries, network or wallet failures, or a change in the value of bitcoin. The limits of liability and dispute terms in the Terms of Service also apply.

11. Privacy and publicity

We use entry information only to run the raffle and pay the prize, as described in the Privacy Policy. When a draw is made, the winning username is announced in the app, including in Global Chat. We will not publish a winner's real name without their permission.

12. Governing law

These Official Rules are governed by the laws of the State of New Hampshire, USA, subject to the dispute terms in the Terms of Service.

13. Winner list and odds

The current odds and draw times are at Resets, Timelines & Odds and in Section 16g of the Terms. The winning username is announced in the app after each draw. To ask which username won a given month's draw, email info@603btc.com within 60 days of that draw.
